# Tee Solar Inverters — research brief

Gathered 2026-10-25 from public search results for TikTok [@teesolarinverter](https://www.tiktok.com/@teesolarinverter).
The profile page itself could not be fetched (TikTok renders client-side and blocks bots), so bio,
follower counts and the full video list are **not verified**. Confirm everything marked (?) with the owner.

## Who they are
- **Brand:** Tee Solar Inverters (hashtag `#teesolarinverters`), solar arm of **DarlingTee Global Limited**.
- **Parent:** DarlingTee Global Limited, CAC RC-7795698, incorporated 2024-08-05, registered in Isheri, Lagos.
  Website [darlingtee.com.ng](https://darlingtee.com.ng/) (503 when checked) says the business started in 2018 and covers phones, solar and e-bikes.
- **Sister accounts:** DarlingTee Phones (TikTok `@darlingteephones`, Threads `@darlingtee_phones`), which sells iPhones. Their slogan is "Sweet sweet deals" (`#sweetsweetdeals`).
- **Location:** DarlingTee Mall, Ijegun, Lagos, Nigeria (walk-in store, orders and pickup).
- **Sales phone/WhatsApp:** 08138526238, used in video captions (e.g. "Let us know how much ur budget is").
  The parent site lists other staff lines (Chisom, Oluchi, Seyi, Ola, a CEO line and a PA line). Numbers vary between sources (?).
- **Warranty:** 1 year on new products (according to the parent site).
- **Audience:** Nigerian homes, small businesses (chemist shops, offices), Starlink users, and resellers ("electronics dealer in Ijebu-Ode").
- **Content style:** product demos, price reveals, "Replying to @…" Q&A, battery comparisons, installation videos, before/after price cuts.

## Product categories
1. **Portable solar generators / power stations** (the main line in recent posts)
   - Brands: **Lutian** (incl. 3000W), **itel Power Tank** (500W/1kWh and 1000/2kWh), **Meco** (1kWh/2kWh),
     **Colar 3600**, **Hicces** all-in-one (3kVA 5kWh / 3kVA 7kWh), **Qasa SPG 500**, **MOLI**, and an unbranded 300W unit that can run Starlink.
   - Claims: some units "can power AC".
2. **Inverters**: hybrid inverters from 3.5kVA to 6kVA, 48V systems, pure sine wave. Brands seen: Felicity, Haisic. Fairly used units are also sold.
3. **Batteries**: lithium-ion (5kWh, 8kWh, 10kWh), tubular and gel. They post comparison content on these.
4. **Solar panels**: 550W and 600W.
5. **Full installation packages** (supply, delivery and installation).

## Website implications
- Catalog grouped by category: Solar Generators, Inverters, Batteries, Panels, Packages.
- Prices shown as "from ₦…" with a WhatsApp CTA on every product. Ordering happens over WhatsApp, not a checkout.
- Budget/load calculator ("What do you want to power?") to match their "tell us your budget" approach.
- Embed TikTok videos, which serve as social proof.
- Store location with a map (DarlingTee Mall, Ijegun), opening hours (?), and installation service area (?).
- Cross-link DarlingTee Phones.
